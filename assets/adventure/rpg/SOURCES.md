## Prompts de creation

### harbour.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game asset for WarsNuto browser RPG,
original illustration, NOT a UI mockup. Create a wide 16:9 hand-painted
anime-style pirate harbour battle arena at dawn, view side-on with slight
perspective: broad empty worn stone quayside foreground for two fighters,
calm teal sea beyond, small wooden sailing boats, white coastal buildings
with red roofs to the far left, sea cliffs on the right, soft bright cloud
sky. Camera close to the ground, horizon at upper third, open and readable
centre. Crisp painterly outlines, rich turquoise, coral and sea green with
ivory light, no dominant dark blue or brown. Clean professional 2D RPG
environment. No characters, no text, no interface, no watermarks, no One Piece
logos or recognizable proprietary places. Original world art, landscape.

### adventurer.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG character sprite for
a 2D pirate RPG browser battle. One original young adult pirate navigator,
full body entirely in frame with roomy clear margins, three-quarter profile
facing RIGHT, feet grounded, athletic battle-ready stance, dark short tousled
hair, white sailor shirt, open short coral red coat with teal sash, dark
trousers and black boots, holds one curved naval sabre in right hand angled
forward. Charming adventurous anime hand-drawn line art, crisp cell shading,
elegant professional game illustration, visually legible at small size.
Original person, not a One Piece character, no straw hat, no text no logos,
no background, no border, genuine transparent background. Must see both feet
and entire sword tip.

## Audio

Les trois fichiers impact.wav, guard.wav et heal.wav de ../audio sont des
compositions procedurales originales generees par
scripts/generate-adventure-sounds.cjs. Les boucles musicales precedentes sont
conservees. Aucun morceau commercial n'a ete telecharge. Le rendu reste
synthetique : une bande originale instrumentale complete reste a produire.
